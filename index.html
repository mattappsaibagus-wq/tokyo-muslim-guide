Tokyo Camii — Japan's Largest Mosque

If you visit only one mosque in Tokyo, make it this one. A masterpiece of Ottoman architecture in the heart of Shibuya.

Iconic Landmark Tours Welcome

Tokyo Camii & Diyanet Turkish Culture Center 東京ジャーミイ

1-19 Oyamacho, Shibuya-ku · Yoyogi-Uehara Sta. (5 min walk) Jumu'ah (Friday prayer) ~13:00 — arrive early, it fills up fast Capacity ≈ 2,000 · Women's prayer hall · Halal shop on site

Built by the Turkish Diyanet foundation, Tokyo Camii welcomes visitors of all faiths outside prayer times. Modest dress required; scarves are available to borrow at the entrance.
