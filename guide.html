Ingredients · 原材料

Japanese Ingredients to Watch

Save this table or take a screenshot — it will help you read labels and menus anywhere in Japan.

Avoid (not halal)

  • 豚肉 / ポーク Pork (butaniku / pōku)
  • ラード Lard (rādo)
  • ゼラチン Gelatin (often pork-derived)
  • 酒 / みりん Sake / mirin (alcohol)
  • コンソメ Consommé (usually pork/chicken mix)
  • ショートニング Shortening (may contain lard)

Generally safe

  • 鮭 / 魚 Salmon / fish (sake* / sakana)
  • 梅 Pickled plum (ume)
  • 野菜 Vegetables (yasai)
  • 卵 Egg (tamago)
  • 米 Rice (kome / gohan)
  • 枝豆 Edamame

*Note: 鮭 (salmon, "sake") and 酒 (alcohol, "sake") share a reading — check the kanji!
